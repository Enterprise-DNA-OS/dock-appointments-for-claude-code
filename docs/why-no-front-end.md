# Why the receiving desk starts with records

The receiving office can book loads, record observed movements and prepare a shift handover from a database and a coding agent. Read-only HTML gives the team a printable plan and evidence record. A new field or question becomes a tested change in the business's own system.

A carrier portal gives external parties self-service booking, authenticated access and notifications. A mobile app gives quick entry on the dock and can support offline capture. A live grid gives dispatchers drag-and-drop changes. This base does not supply those surfaces. It is designed for an office that can operate the schedule itself. Enterprise DNA can scope portal access, phone capture and integrations with the owner, including the identity and safety controls they require.

The database is not a physical interlock. Keep site controls, vehicle movements and load decisions with competent people. Drafted correspondence stays local until a person reviews and sends it.
